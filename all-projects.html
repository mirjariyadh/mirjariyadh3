Project Requirements Guide

Not sure what information to send for a BIM estimate? Here is what helps speed up quote calculation:

1. Source Drawings or Scan Data 2D CAD (.dwg), PDF blueprints, sketches, or 3D Laser Scan point clouds (.e57, .rcp, .las).
2. Target LOD (Level of Development) LOD 200 (Schematic), LOD 300 (Design/Permit), LOD 350 (Coordinated), or LOD 400 (Fabrication).
3. Project Units & Standards Imperial (Feet/Inches) or Metric (Millimeters/Meters), along with regional building codes.
4. Scope of Disciplines Architectural exterior/interior, Structural frame, MEP (HVAC ducting, plumbing, electrical, fire protection).
5. Required Deliverable Formats Autodesk Revit (.RVT), openBIM (.IFC), Navisworks (.NWD), AutoCAD (.DWG), or PDF sheets.
6. Timeline & Milestones Target completion deadline and any phased delivery requirements.
